# Legal & Privacy Notes

## Current portfolio behavior
- Static portfolio hosted on Netlify
- No analytics configured in the source
- No marketing or advertising trackers
- No portfolio cookies or localStorage usage
- No external Google Fonts; system font stack only
- External services are reached only after visitors follow project/GitHub links

## Before public deployment
1. Fill the serviceable postal address in `src/imprint.html`.
2. Fill the operator contact email in `src/imprint.html`.
3. Mirror those controller details in `src/privacy.html`.
4. Verify Netlify site settings have not enabled analytics or other tracking independently of this repository.
5. Rebuild and manually test `/imprint.html` and `/privacy.html`.

## Consent banner rule
Do not add a cosmetic cookie banner while there is nothing optional to consent to. If optional analytics/marketing/device-storage technologies are introduced, perform a new privacy review and implement prior consent where legally required.

## Scope
This repository provides technical structure, not individualized legal advice. Requirements can vary with the operator's status and future site functionality.
